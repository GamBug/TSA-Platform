## Remaining editorial decisions

- Ambiguous mathematical domains and conventions are made explicit: digit repetition, one vehicle for the whole journey,
  labelled groups, unit-square counting, the zero-volume boundary case, and a geometry model rather than unverified
  real-building dimensions. Coordinate identities, conditional probabilities and trig domains were checked directly.
- Exact response formats now match the task: a unique maximal bounded increasing interval, two ordered coordinate fields,
  and an explicitly rounded decimal length instead of mixing exact and rounded accepted values.
- The graph question follows the visible minimum inside the stated interval. The rational-function statement now says
  decreasing on each separate interval, and its truth value changes accordingly. The expansion with negative powers is
  written as a finite Laurent sum with its actual exponent range.
- Reading completion questions use clear phrase banks, matching length instructions and grammatical blanks. Passage-based
  choices explicitly require support from the passage, rather than unstated outside inference.
- Science questions distinguish explicitly named research subjects from overlapping biological categories, residual-starch
  detection from proof of complete glucose formation, and a catalyst's actual function from an incomplete sentence.
- The radiation completion compares the stated characteristics and penetration, replacing the blanket claim of greater
  danger. The decay problem declares a hypothetical mathematical threshold and the smallest integer day; it no longer
  presents a made-up discharge regulation. Duplicate nursing statements become distinct conditional time/distance items.
- Mussel questions use conditions actually described in the passage, without unsupported precise water speeds or an
  optimum claim. Rapid reproduction replaces inferred age at maturity. The passage identifies its graph as mussel-density
  data, not chlorophyll data. The wave relation and starch mass balance were also corrected in shared passages.

There are six answer-representation changes in the private manifest. Four adjust accepted formats or field structure;
two accompany changed propositions. No original answer record or historical exam is edited.

been approved. Publishing those parents could overwrite a corrected eligible pointer. The regression first failed with
“an unpublished parent cannot overwrite its successor.” The selector now excludes any version with a newer sibling of the
same question. Tests confirm that the latest version is published while its old parent stays DRAFT and unselectable.

this filter does not automatically withhold their four defective originals, and the report makes that limit explicit.
